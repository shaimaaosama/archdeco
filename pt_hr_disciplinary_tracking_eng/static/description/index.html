Module Purpose

This module supports HR Disciplinary Tracking business workflow operations. It introduces and extends records around discipline category, category line, and disciplinary action to support daily business operations. It provides operational interfaces such as Search information, Group By, Department.

Key Features

  • Business screens and actions including Search information, Group By, Department, Discipline Categories.
  • Dedicated data models for discipline category, category line, and disciplinary action.
  • Configuration and master-data controls to match organization policy.

How To Use

  4. Review statuses, calculations, and approvals according to your process.
  5. Use reports/views to monitor outcomes and operational progress.
