Detailed Features

01 Seamlessly filters contacts in Sales, Purchase, and Invoicing modules based on the Is Customer or Is Vendor checkbox in the contact form.
02 Eliminates confusion by ensuring that only customers appear in the customer fields (sales and purchase) and only vendors appear in vendor fields (purchases and Bills).
03 Works uniformly across:

  • Sales (Sales Order, Quotations)
  • Purchase (RFQs, Purchase Orders)
  • Invoicing (Customer Invoices, Vendor Bills)
04 Keeps your contact database clean and well-structured by clearly separating customers and vendor roles, reducing the risk of misassigned transactions.
05 Does not alter the default user experience - instead, it enhances the native contact form with additional checkboxes that trigger smart backend filtering.
06 Especially useful for companies dealing with a large number of contacts, helping streamline operations and boost efficiency in sales, procurement, and accounting workflows.

Set Up:

Download & install the app from Odoo App Store.
Go to Contacts >> Open a contact >> Navigate to Sales & Purchase tab
Select the checkbox for either Is Customer or Is Vendor.
You're now ready to use the smart filters inside:

  • Sales >> Orders

  • Purchase >> Orders/RFQs

  • Invoicing >> Customer Invoices/Vendor Bills

Work Flow

Open the Sales, Purchase, or Invoicing module. For now we will open Sales module.
You will now only see relevant contacts in each dropdown - customers and vendors are fully bifurcated.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Will this work in both Sales and Purchase modules?

Yes. The app intelligently filters customer field entries in Sales and vendor field entries in Purchase, based on your contact settings.

Can this replace my current client database software?

Absolutely. This module works as a lightweight customer database software or customer info management system right inside Odoo.

Does it support invoice-related filtering?

Yes. All invoice software views and fields in Odoo's Invoicing app will follow the bifurcation between customers and vendors for cleaner data entry.
